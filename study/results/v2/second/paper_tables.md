# Tables for the two new corpora

Written by `v2/second/paper_tables.py` from the committed results in `results/v2/second/`. Do not edit by hand.

Results found: Holmes for Qwen3-32B, Qwen3-8B, Llama 3.1 8B, Llama 3.3 70B; remakes for Qwen3-32B, Qwen3-8B, Llama 3.1 8B, Llama 3.3 70B. Qwen3-32B is the primary model: it decides every test; the others are replications.

A p of 0.00001 from 100,000 random draws, or of 0.0001 from 10,000 random draws, is the smallest that null can give. The noise-calibrated p of the decision test cannot be smaller than 1 in (1 + the number of pure-noise runs).

Rounding: a number is rounded once, from the unrounded value in the file. A p under 0.001 is written out to two significant digits; a p that would round to 1.00 is written to three decimals; a p that would round to exactly 0.05 is given more digits. Where rounding would print an AUC as one of the plan's levels (0.80, 0.70; 0.65 for the lower bound of the interval) although it is under that level, the unrounded value is given beside it.
