# UN-27: Steered in Qwen, read in Llama

**Area:** Across models · **Status:** design registered 8 November 2026, not run · **Priority:** 4.0 of 5

## Question

When Qwen is steered to write a meme with its usual words blocked, does the matching Llama unit detect it in the text?

## Prediction

For 100 Qwen units with beyond-word twins, passages written under steering (the unit's activation raised during writing) are traced to the right unit by Llama's twin readings at 20% top-1 or better (chance 1%), and at half or more of that rate when each unit's ten best words are banned from the output.

## What would refute it

Top-1 is under 5% with words banned, or no better than for matched Qwen units that lack a beyond-word twin, steered the same way. Then twin agreement does not survive when the meme is set by intervention. 100 units by 20 passages give 2,000 trials and a standard error under 1 point.

## Data

Generated by the experiment, no outside data: for each of 100 Qwen3-8B units (from UN21's list and the study's labelled concept units), 20 short story continuations from neutral prompts with the unit raised, the same for 100 matched comparison units, and unsteered controls. About 6,000 passages of 60 words, released under CC BY 4.0.

## Method

Ground truth: which unit was raised. Qwen writes; Llama only reads. Each passage is assigned to the unit whose Llama twin responds most, scaled by the unsteered controls. Word ban: the unit's ten best words (UN21's lasso) cannot be output. Steering strength is set on separate development units, and a blind LLM check confirms the text stays fluent.

## Cheap rival it must beat

Given half the passages for training: a TF-IDF classifier, a sentence-embedding classifier, and an LLM reader shown the unit labels. Also word overlap with each unit's top sentences, passage length, and Llama's pooled residual stream. TF-IDF is most dangerous without the ban, the embedding classifier with it. Twins get no training passages, so a tie favours twins.

## Premise it tests

Both. The only test in this area where the meme in a text is known by construction. A pass shows a unit means the same thing when written by one model and read by another. A fail alongside a pass in UN21 means twins share inputs, not meaning.

## Biology analogue

A gene moved into another species and still expressed there.

## Cost

S to M. A steering hook is a few lines in the existing recorder (`study/extract.py`); about five hours of generation on the laptop, minutes of reading, and a small LLM check.

## Payoff

Causal evidence for or against P2 at unit level, and the first steering result of any kind in this project. Cammarata's essay uses steering as the direct test of a unit's label, and the study never ran it.

## Main risk

Units that read a concept often do not write it, and raising a single unit in a 4-bit 8B model may produce nothing or nonsense; the share of steerable units is itself a result. With words banned, steering may fail outright, leaving nothing for Llama to read.

## Reviewer note

Added: no design in the area had a causal check, and this seed was the only one with ground truth set by intervention. Remaining weakness: single-unit steering may fail on most units, and steering transfer between models by learned maps is already reported (Agarwal 2026), though not this reading test.

## Cross-area ranking

In the top twelve of 1 of 3 judges (speed and cost: rank 11). Once UN-21's unit list and EN-21's hook exist it costs about five hours of generation and is the only causal test of agreement between the two models.
