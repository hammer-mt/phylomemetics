## Neurons as units (P1)

- **A** [Cammarata 2026, Neural Networks Are Already Clean (Goodfire; published 15 Oct 2026, revised 30 Oct 2026)](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/). The source of P1. Read the footnotes and the appendix, not the summary. Its technical companion came out on 10 November 2026 (next entry)
- **A** [Tigges, Kowal, McGrath, Lewis, Cammarata 2026, Neurons as an Interpretable Basis (Goodfire research post, 10 Nov 2026; not peer reviewed)](https://www.goodfire.com/research/neurons-as-an-interpretable-basis). The technical companion to the essay. One 35B model, 5,468 sampled neuron poles (a pole is the positive or negative side of one neuron): at strong activation an LLM judge found one role in 47% and one role or one dominant role in 69%. Descriptions predict strong firing. No word-count, embedding or LLM-reader rival, no rewording test, and no second model beyond a case study of one neuron
- **A** [Barez, Wu and 25 co-authors 2026, Understanding AI from the Inside: The State of Mechanistic Interpretability for AI Safety (arXiv:2611.08438; 182 pages as first posted on 5 Nov 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08438). The field's own statement of where both premises stand. Learned features are its default unit; superposition is 'a hypothesis' in large models; shared algorithms are supported and shared representations less so. Read sections 4.2, 5, 6.5, 7.5, 9.1, 11, 12.6 and 14
- **A** [Nanda 2026, LessWrong comment on the linkpost](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/x4kvabkF2Jk8GLZp8/linkpost-neural-networks-are-already-clean/comment/7wcSqBNzQF2o9SHyY). The main expert rebuttal: facts, weak activations, loose definition
- **A** [IRSI 2026, retest note (AI-agent-written, not peer reviewed)](https://irsi.ai/notes/already-clean/). Only outside numbers: 78%, 57 to 65% vs 31% random directions; planted-mixture blind spot
- **A** [Arora, Wu, Steinhardt, Schwettmann 2026, Language Model Circuits Are Sparse in the Neuron Basis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22594). Peer-reviewed support for the neuron basis in Llama 3.1 8B; shows sets, not single neurons
- **A** [Choi, Huang, Meng, Johnson, Steinhardt, Schwettmann 2024, Scaling Automatic Neuron Description (Transluce, 23 Oct 2024)](https://transluce.org/neuron-descriptions). Descriptions and scores for every neuron of one of the study's models
- **A** [Gurnee, Nanda, Pauly, Harvey, Troitskii, Bertsimas 2023, Finding Neurons in a Haystack: Case Studies with Sparse Probing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01610). Layer and scale conditions; k-sparse probing method for seed 4
- **A** [Gurnee et al. 2024, Universal Neurons in GPT2 Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12181). 1 to 5% universal: the base rate for P2 at neuron level
- **A** [Bolukbasi et al. 2021, An Interpretability Illusion for BERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.07143). Top examples depend on the corpus; applies to in-corpus labelling
- **A** [Paulo & Belrose 2025, SAEs Trained on the Same Data Learn Different Features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16615). Seed-dependence of SAE units
- **A** [Shabalin and Piotrowski 2026, Comparing Sparsified MLPs and Transcoders: Many Transcoder Features Are Neurons in Disguise (workshop paper)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24521). In 13 public transcoders (learned sparse stand-ins for a model's MLP layers), features that copy one neuron are a substantial share of active late-layer features. When the authors trained their own twice, most features that recurred were these neuron copies. Support for the raw neuron as a citable unit
- **A** [Voita, Ferrando, Nalmpantis 2024, Neurons in Large Language Models: Dead, N-gram, Positional (Findings of ACL)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.75/). In models up to 66B parameters many early-layer neurons are token and n-gram detectors and some fire by position. The word-level rival, found inside the neurons themselves: screen these out before calling a unit a meme
- **B** [Goodfire 2026, Neuron Atlas for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (public sample of 3,000 neuron poles)](https://www.goodfire.com/research/public_neuron_atlas). Descriptions, roles, examples and 'not this' cases for 3,000 poles of a model this project has not used. A vocabulary and a labelling format to copy. No data licence is stated, and Goodfire's website terms allow personal, non-commercial use only and forbid automated copying: ask before bulk use
- **B** [Elhage et al. 2022, Toy Models of Superposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.10652). Standard definition; privileged basis
- **B** [Bills et al. 2023, Language models can explain neurons in language models](https://openaipublic.blob.core.windows.net/neuron-explainer/paper/index.html). The 2023 baseline the essay overturns; simulation scoring
- **B** [Bricken et al. 2023, Towards Monosemanticity](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/monosemantic-features/index.html). Origin of the SAE turn; the "four unrelated things" neuron
- **B** [Templeton et al. 2024, Scaling Monosemanticity](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2024/scaling-monosemanticity/index.html). SAE features at scale; feature splitting
- **B** [StefanHex 2026, LessWrong comment](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/x4kvabkF2Jk8GLZp8/linkpost-neural-networks-are-already-clean/comment/bySRD2nBT2GLfPmJS). Computation-in-superposition objection; max-activation caveat
- **B** [Chanin et al. 2024, A is for Absorption](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14507). SAE units are not clean either
- **B** [Leask et al. 2025, SAEs Do Not Find Canonical Units of Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04878). No unit is canonical
- **B** [Kantamneni et al. 2025, Are Sparse Autoencoders Useful?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16681). SAEs lose to simple baselines on probing; the same "beat the cheap rival" lesson
- **B** [Sharkey et al. 2025, Open Problems in Mechanistic Interpretability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16496). Field consensus that the unit question is open
- **B** [Jafari, Eberle, Khakzar, Nanda 2025, RelP: Faithful and Efficient Circuit Discovery in Language Models via Relevance Patching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21258). The attribution tool the Goodfire paper uses to rank neurons by their estimated effect on a prediction. A cheap way to choose units by effect instead of by top activation
- **B** [Feucht, Haklay, Bhalla and 9 others 2026, Arithmetic in the Wild: Llama uses Base-10 Addition to Reason About Cyclic Concepts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01148). A causal neuron-level result in Llama 3.1 8B, one of this project's models: 28 MLP neurons in one layer are reused across tasks. Their job is a step of addition, the kind of operation a label read from top sentences may not reveal
- **B** [Klindt, O'Neill, Reizinger, Maurer, Miolane 2026, A unifying framework from neural superposition to sparse interpretable codes (Nature Machine Intelligence)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01259-z). The peer-reviewed statement of the opposing view: ideas are mixed across neurons and must be unmixed by sparse coding. Read it as the case against P1
- **B** [Colin, Goetschalckx, Fel, Boutin, Serre, Oliver 2026, Choosing the right basis for interpretability: Psychophysical comparison between neuron-based and dictionary-based representations (arXiv version 2, March 2026; first posted in 2024 under another title)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03993). In three experiments with 481 people on two image networks, dictionary units were judged more interpretable than single neurons. Human-rater evidence against P1, in vision models
- **B** [Mu and Andreas 2020, Compositional Explanations of Neurons (NeurIPS)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14032). Neurons in language-inference models learn shallow word-level shortcuts. Offers labels built from AND, OR and NOT of concepts, which fit a neuron with several roles better than one phrase
- **B** [Geva, Caciularu, Wang, Goldberg 2022, Transformer Feed-Forward Layers Build Predictions by Promoting Concepts in the Vocabulary Space (EMNLP)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14680). Gives every neuron a second, free label: the words its output weights promote. Where that disagrees with the label from its top sentences, the label should not be trusted
- **B** [Leavitt and Morcos 2020, Selectivity considered harmful: evaluating the causal impact of class selectivity in DNNs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.01262). Image networks trained to have far less class-selective units kept or nearly kept their accuracy, and networks pushed to be more selective got worse. Selectivity in single units is neither needed nor enough for what a network does. The study's units were never removed or steered, so they are selective readings and nothing more
- **B** [Lecomte, Thaman, Schaeffer, Bashkansky, Chow, Koyejo 2024, What Causes Polysemanticity? An Alternative Origin Story of Mixed Selectivity from Incidental Causes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03096). Neurons that mix concepts can arise by chance even when there are enough neurons for every feature. A mixed neuron is not proof of superposition
- **B** [Nikankin, Reusch, Mueller, Belinkov 2025, Arithmetic Without Algorithms: Language Models Solve Math With a Bag of Heuristics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21272). Inside an arithmetic circuit a sparse set of neurons each apply a simple rule. One of two works the survey cites for the MLP hidden layer being more interpretable as it stands
- **B** [Gao, Rajaram, Coxon, Govande, Baker, Mossing 2025, Weight-sparse transformers have interpretable circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13653). Cleaner neurons obtained by design, at a cost in capability. The contrast case for the claim that ordinary models are already clean
- **B** [Liu, Ponnusamy, Cai, Guo, Kim, Athiwaratkun 2024, Training-Free Activation Sparsity in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14690). On dense Llama and Mistral models from 7B to 70B, 40 to 50% of activations across the model can be zeroed with little loss of performance. The yardstick for whether Goodfire's 92% carries over to dense models; cited in its sparsity section
- **C** [Hänni et al. 2024, Mathematical Models of Computation in Superposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05451). Theory behind "more computations than neurons"
- **C** [Scherlis et al. 2022, Polysemanticity and Capacity in Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01892). Capacity account of polysemanticity
- **C** [GDM mech interp team 2025 (Smith, Rajamanoharan, Conmy, McDougall, Lieberum, Kramár, Shah, Nanda), Negative Results for SAEs On Downstream Tasks and Deprioritising SAE Research (26 Mar 2025)](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/4uXCAJNuPKtKBsi28/negative-results-for-saes-on-downstream-tasks). Why the field was already moving off SAEs
- **C** [Dunefsky et al. 2024, Transcoders Find Interpretable LLM Feature Circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11944). Transcoder alternative
- **C** [Paulo et al. 2025, Transcoders Beat Sparse Autoencoders for Interpretability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18823). Same
- **C** [Liu, Miao, Zhao, Liu, Du 2026, NeuronScope: A Multi-Agent Framework for Explaining Polysemantic Neurons in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03671). Field still assumed polysemantic neurons in Jan 2026
- **C** [Heaven 2026, "'We were looking at it wrong': The explosive claim that's upended the AI world", MIT Technology Review, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/19/1147149/we-were-looking-at-it-wrong-the-explosive-claim-thats-upended-the-ai-world/). Outside reactions (Potts, Hilton, Etzioni)
- **C** [Birardi 2026, Do we need sparsity afterall? (LessWrong, 6 Jan 2026)](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Hk8tCLZKdAPHdoSdb/do-we-need-sparsity-afterall). Small-model neurons are dense; weak but on point
- **C** [Mendel 2024, LessWrong post "SAE feature geometry is outside the superposition hypothesis" (24 June 2024) and Adam Scherlis's comment on it (the quoted comment is dated 6 Jan 2026, id 2BwacbvQkLiqz9Ljn)](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/MFBTjb2qf3ziWmzz6/sae-feature-geometry-is-outside-the-superposition-hypothesis). "Related things are near each other" predates the essay
- **C** [Chang and Bergen 2025, Bigram Subnetworks: Mapping to Next Tokens in Transformer Language Models (NeurIPS)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15471). In models up to 1B parameters, the part of the network that predicts the next word from the current word alone sits mostly in the first MLP layer. Early-layer neurons should be tested separately before any claim that a neuron follows an idea
- **C** [Fishman, Chmiel, Banner, Soudry 2024, Scaling FP8 training to trillion-token LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12517). Low-precision (8-bit) training exposes outliers amplified by SwiGLU, the gated activation that Qwen and Llama models use. A study run at 4-bit should check its extreme neuron readings against full precision

## Agreement between models (P2)

NeurIPS 2025 poster; arXiv:2505.12540, https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12540. The P2 citation; sentence encoders only; 3 of 15 seeds converge for the one cross-backbone pair tested for seed stability (e5 to gtr)

In language models up to 30B parameters, neurons shared between independently trained models grow in number but shrink as a share of all neurons, and become more selective. The scaling law for P2 at neuron level
- **B** [Chen, Merullo, Stolfo, Pavlick 2025, Transferring Linear Features Across Language Models With Model Stitching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06609). - **B** [Oozeer et al. 2025, Activation Space Interventions Can Be Transferred Between LLMs. arXiv:2503.04429,](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/oozeer25a.html). Steering transfer across Llama, Qwen, Gemma (0.5B to 3B instruction-tuned models; mostly small-to-large within a family)
